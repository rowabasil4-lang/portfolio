About

I am an Information and Communications Engineering graduate of Al-Nahrain University in Baghdad, and I designed alongside the degree the whole way through. The engineering side taught me structure. The design side is where I get to decide what someone notices first.

Most of my work is for small businesses and educational pages: logos and visual identities, promotional posters, advertising cards, social media sets. I take a brief from a client who often cannot yet describe what they want, and turn it into something they recognise the moment they see it.

I also teach mathematics in Arabic to primary and intermediate students at Abwab Educational Platform. Standing in front of a class that has stopped following you teaches you more about visual hierarchy than any tutorial does, and a lot of my educational design work comes out of it.

I trained in graphic design at Makers of Baghdad, and in web development through the Zain Iraq Women in Tech programme, which means I can hand a developer files that actually make sense on screen.
